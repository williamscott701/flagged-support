Flagged

Minesweeper, drawn as a survey map.

About the game

Flagged is classic minesweeper for iPhone and iPad. Open every safe cell; the numbers tell you how many of the eight touching cells hide a mine. Plant a surveyor's pennant where you know a mine lies.

  • Beginner (9 × 9, 10 mines), Intermediate (16 × 16, 40 mines), Expert (30 × 16, 99 mines) and Custom fields.
  • The first step is always safe and always opens a clearing.
  • No guessing: fields checked by a logical solver so they can always be cleared without a guess.
  • A daily field, the same for every player, with a streak.
  • Long press or the Reveal / Flag switch to plant pennants; tap a satisfied number to clear around it.
  • Pinch to zoom and pan on large fields; keyboard and trackpad play on iPad.
  • Statistics for every sheet: games played, won, best time and streaks. Fields in progress are saved.

Free

All of Flagged is free: every sheet, custom fields, the daily field and no-guessing fields. There are no ads, no purchases, no subscription and no account.

Help

How do I flag a cell? Long press it, or switch the control under the field to Flag and tap. On iPad you can also right click or press F.

What does "No guessing" mean? Before you start, each new field is played through by a solver that never guesses. Only fields it can clear by logic are used.

Can I turn on question marks? Yes, in Settings. Marking then cycles pennant, question mark, nothing.
