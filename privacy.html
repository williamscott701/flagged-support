Flagged · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026.

The short version

Flagged has no account, no ads and no tracking. It sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, which are not linked to you, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your statistics, your expedition progress, today's daily field, the field in progress and a note of which Survey Charter or trial your Apple Account holds are stored only on your device, so the game can resume where you left off. They are removed when you delete the app.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device, one a day at the time you choose. They are created by your device, not sent from a server, and no notification token is collected. The app asks for notification permission only when you choose to turn the reminder on.

Share cards and Photos

When you share a result, the card is drawn on your device and goes only where you send it through the share sheet. If you save a card to Photos, the app asks for permission to add photos only; it never reads your photo library.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which levels are too hard, how the daily field is played and how the trial and plans are used, the app sends anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. For example: a level was started or completed (with its number, its time and its survey marks), the daily field was played, the plan screen was opened, a plan was chosen or bought, a setting was changed, the daily reminder was turned on (with its hour), a card was shared or saved (with its kind and shape, and the app it was shared to). It also sends broad groupings such as "trial", "yearly" or "level 11–25".

  • No name, email, account, user ID, advertising ID or vendor ID is sent, and nothing you could be identified by is added to the events. Google Analytics keeps a random app-instance identifier for this installation of the app, so that events from the same installation can be counted together; it is not linked to you and is replaced if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • The statistics are not linked to you and are not used for advertising or to track you across apps and websites. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are always switched off.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage stays denied, so Google receives only cookieless, identifier-free measurements.
  • Like any internet service, Google receives your device's IP address when the events are sent and uses it to derive a rough region; Google's processing is covered by Google's own privacy policy.
  • To stop it, turn off Settings › Field notes › Share anonymous statistics. Nothing more is sent from that moment, including on later launches.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • No account, no sign-in and no personal information requested.
  • No advertising or tracking code is included in the app. The only third-party code is Google Analytics for Firebase, described above.

Purchases

The free trial, the subscriptions and the lifetime charter are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account. The app asks the App Store, on your device, which of them your account holds; the developer receives no personal information from these purchases. The anonymous statistics record that a plan was bought and its price, never who bought it. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app asks no one for personal information, children included. The anonymous statistics are the same for every player and can be switched off in Settings.
