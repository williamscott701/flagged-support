Flagged · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026.

The short version

Flagged collects no data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your statistics, today's daily field and the field in progress are stored only on your device so the game can resume where you left off. They are removed when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • No account, no sign-in and no personal information requested.
  • No third-party code or services are included in the app.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

The 7-day trial and the full-game purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the trial or the full game is owned on this account.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: williamscott701@gmail.com.
