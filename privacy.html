Flagged · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026.

The short version

Flagged collects no data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your statistics, your expedition progress, today's daily field, the field in progress and a note of which Survey Charter or trial your Apple Account holds are stored only on your device, so the game can resume where you left off. They are removed when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • No account, no sign-in and no personal information requested.
  • No third-party code or services are included in the app.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own; it only asks Apple's App Store about your purchases.

Purchases

The free trial, the subscriptions and the lifetime charter are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account. The app asks the App Store, on your device, which of them your account holds; the developer receives no personal information from these purchases. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Because no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

Changes and contact
