The 15-day free trial

  • The trial is an in-app purchase at no charge, named "15-day Trial". It starts when you tap Start on the app's first screen and lasts 15 days (360 hours) from that moment.

The lifetime charter

  • The lifetime charter is a one-time in-app purchase that gives access to the whole game for good. It does not renew.
